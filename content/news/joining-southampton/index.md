I am very happy to share that I have taken up a post as **Lecturer in Palaeolithic Archaeology** in the [**Department of Archaeology**](https://www.southampton.ac.uk/study/subjects/archaeology) at the University of Southampton, where I am a member of the [**Centre for the Archaeology of Human Origins**](https://caho.soton.ac.uk/).

Alongside my research across Mediterranean Europe, the Levant, and southern Africa, I am teaching across the undergraduate programme and at MA level. Details of all my modules are on my [teaching page](/teaching), and I am [open to enquiries](/supervision) from prospective students and from early-career researchers considering fellowship applications.
